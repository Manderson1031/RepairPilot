# RepairPilot Private Beta Terms — Product Draft

RepairPilot provides informational diagnostic assistance for equipment repair. It may be incomplete or incorrect.

Users must follow equipment manufacturer instructions, lockout/tagout procedures, and applicable workplace rules. Red/high-risk procedures should be handled by a qualified technician. RepairPilot must not be used to bypass guards, interlocks, emergency stops, or other safety systems.

The beta may change, experience downtime, or lose non-production data. Testers should independently verify critical specifications and procedures.

This is a product-development draft and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before commercial/public release.
