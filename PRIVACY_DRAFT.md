# RepairPilot Privacy Notice — Beta Draft

RepairPilot stores account information, equipment profiles, uploaded equipment photos/manuals, diagnostic answers, repair history, feedback, and limited security/audit events to provide and improve the service.

## Important beta principles
- Equipment data belongs to the account that uploaded it.
- API credentials and cloud service keys remain server-side.
- Uploaded manuals/photos should be kept in private storage.
- Users can request a JSON export through the account export endpoint.
- Users can delete their account; V11 also deletes the user's stored photo/manual objects through the configured storage provider.
- RepairPilot should collect only telemetry needed to operate, secure, debug, and improve the beta.

This is a product-development draft, not legal advice. Before public launch, the final notice should identify the operating legal entity, contact method, subprocessors, retention periods, jurisdiction-specific rights, and app-store disclosures.
