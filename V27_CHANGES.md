# RepairPilot V27 — Visual Master Pass

V27 rebuilds the mobile presentation around the approved industrial RepairPilot visual specification.

- Dark charcoal/black industrial visual system with high-contrast amber accents.
- Compact branded top bar and persistent five-item bottom navigation.
- Login rebuilt around the RepairPilot gear/tool identity.
- Home rebuilt as a compact technician dashboard.
- Hardware Scanner and AR Repair Assistant promoted to core product capabilities.
- Equipment rebuilt as a searchable compact list; add-equipment moved behind the + control.
- AI Diagnosis rebuilt with selected-equipment summary and 4-stage visual stepper while preserving the existing diagnostic engine, photos, manuals, safety stop, pause/resume, and repair completion flow.
- Hardware Scanner UI is represented in the visual master but remains intentionally labeled/treated as a future functional engine until camera/depth measurement is implemented and validated.
- No fake user equipment or fake repair history is injected into the live app.
- Visual master asset: mobile/assets/repairpilot-v27-design-master.png
- Mobile version: 0.19.0; iOS/Android build: 19; package: 0.12.0.

The visual master is a design specification, not a screenshot claiming unimplemented features are functional.
